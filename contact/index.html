Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book?

Ideally 2–4 weeks before you arrive, and earlier for July–October (high season).

What languages do your guides speak?

English, French and Malagasy. Tours are guided in English unless you ask otherwise.

Can you build a custom itinerary?

Yes. Every trip can be adapted to your dates, budget and interests.

How do I pay?

A 30% deposit confirms your booking, 40% is due 30 days before departure and the balance on arrival. Full details are in our Booking Conditions.
