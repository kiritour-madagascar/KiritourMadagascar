Madagascar Itinerary: How to Plan Your Trip from Morondava

Morondava is the gateway to western Madagascar's icons. Here's how to plan a 3, 5 or 7+ day itinerary covering baobabs, Tsingy, Kirindy and the river.

Morondava, on Madagascar's west coast, is the perfect base for exploring the wonders of the Menabe region — the Avenue of the Baobabs, Kirindy Forest, Tsingy de Bemaraha and the Tsiribihina River. How you combine them depends on how many days you have. Here's how to plan.

Short Trip: 1-2 Days

With limited time, focus on the icons closest to Morondava. A day tour to the Avenue of the Baobabs at sunset, combined with the nearby Kimony beach and Betania fishing village, gives a strong taste of the region. Add a day trip to Kirindy Forest for your first lemurs and a chance at spotting a fossa.

Classic Trip: 3-4 Days

Three to four days is enough to reach Tsingy de Bemaraha, the UNESCO stone forest. A typical loop drives from Morondava to Bekopaka (with a baobabs stop), explores the Grand and Petit Tsingy, then returns via Kirindy Forest for wildlife and a final baobabs sunset.

Complete Trip: 5-8 Days

With a week, you can add the Tsiribihina River descent to the circuit, combining a peaceful pirogue journey with the Tsingy, Kirindy and baobabs. This is the most complete western Madagascar experience, blending river, stone forest, wildlife and iconic landscapes.

Local tip

Western Madagascar's roads are rough and remote, with river ferry crossings. A private 4x4 with a local guide saves time, handles logistics and is far safer than going independently.

When to Visit

Plan your trip in the dry season, April to November. The roads to Tsingy and Kirindy are only reliably passable in these months, and the weather is best for the baobabs at sunset. The rainy season can cut off access to the interior entirely.

How to Get to Morondava

Morondava has a small airport with flights from Antananarivo, or you can travel overland via Antsirabe and Miandrivazo. Once you arrive, a guided tour takes care of all transport and accommodation so you can focus on the experience rather than the logistics.

Frequently asked questions

At least 2 days for the baobabs and Kirindy, 3–4 days to add the Tsingy, and 5–8 days for the full loop with the Tsiribihina River.
